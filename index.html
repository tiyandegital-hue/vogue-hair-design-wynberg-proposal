The 7 Critical Structural Findings

Probe date: 2026-11-06 · Domain: voguehairdesign.co.za

Finding · 01[sitemap.xml — 1 URL — observed 2026-11-06]

“One page does all the work”

The site only utilizes #services and #team in-page anchor fragments. There are zero dedicated indexable landing pages for balayage, ammonia-free colour (Davines), treatments (KERAMESS, Mycro Keratin, Botox, permanent straightener), kids cuts, bridal, or key Southern Suburbs.

Algorithmic consequence

Google and LLM crawlers are forced to evaluate a single URL against dozens of specialized search queries. When a client in Constantia searches for “ammonia free Davines specialist”, Vogue cannot rank against multi-page salon competitors.

1 URL · every query! Critical bottleneck

Finding · 02[FAQ matches on site: 0]

“Invisible to AI answers”

Zero FAQ content and zero FAQPage JSON-LD schema found anywhere in DOM scans.

Algorithmic consequence

Google AI Overviews and Search “People Also Ask” quote competitors who directly answer clients' questions. Vogue answers zero.

FAQ: 0 · FAQPage schema: 0Zero citations

Finding · 03['testimonial' on site: 0]

“55 reviews, zero voices”

Displays only a static badge reading “4.8 · 55 Reviews”. No quote text, no client stories, and zero Review or AggregateRating schema on quote cards.

Algorithmic consequence

Decades of customer devotion earned offline remain trapped and unreachable to potential high-ticket clients browsing from Constantia and Kenilworth.

Trust conversion: LostUnindexed proof

Finding · 04[Book Now destination: fresha.com]

“Every booking leaks to Fresha”

Every single call-to-action button redirects instantly off-domain to fresha.com. The Vogue domain surrenders attribution tracking, first-party cookie context, and cross-session pixel data.

What Vogue gives away

  • Retention remarketing access
  • Conversion attribution in GA4
  • Seamless on-site luxury experience

What Fresha gains

  • Vogue's high-value client relationships
  • Freedom to recommend competing salons
  • Zero platform accountability to Vogue

Domain leakage: 100% of CTAsConversion drain

Geo radius

No Suburb Footprint

Vogue serves wealthy surrounding Southern Suburbs, but search engines have no textual anchor linking Vogue to the neighbouring catchments.

Local pack radius: Restricted to Wynberg core

Finding · 06[http:// response: 200 OK (no 301 to https)]

Protocol

Insecure HTTP Serves Live

Unencrypted traffic is served alongside HTTPS without a permanent redirect rule, diluting domain authority and risking browser warnings.

Status: Dual hostnames live in crawler indexes

Finding · 07[Indexed legacy URL active, main site bridal: 0]

Canonical

Zombie 'Copy of Vogue Brides' Indexed

A draft URL is indexed and ranking on Google, while the official homepage lacks bridal architecture entirely.

Action: 301 redirect or prune zombie entity
